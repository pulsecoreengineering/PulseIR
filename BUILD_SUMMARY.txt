  1️⃣  "IR is the architecture, not YAML"
      └─ PulseModel is reusable; YAML is just serialization

  2️⃣  "Stable core, extensible domains"
      └─ HSM core (states, events) fixed; domains (sensors, drivers) plug-in

  3️⃣  "Validation early, generation dumb"
      └─ Parser is strict; codegen assumes valid input

  4️⃣  "No format fragmentation"
      └─ Single IR unifies PulseCore IDE, PulseSim, PulseDash
